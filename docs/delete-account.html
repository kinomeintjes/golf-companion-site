Delete your Golf Companion account

Golf Companion by Kino Meintjes

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Golf Companion and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete my account under Sign out, and confirm.

Without the app

Email kinomeintjes@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We'll confirm within 7 days.

What's deleted

Your account, your rounds and handicap history, your profile and friend code, your friendships and the games you created — permanently.

What stays

Courses you added to the shared course library stay available to other golfers, without your name attached. Rounds saved on your phone without an account stay on that phone until you delete them or uninstall the app.
