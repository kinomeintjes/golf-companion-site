Put Golf Companion on your Garmin watch

GPS distances to the green, hazards, a club for every shot and your scorecard, on your wrist. It takes about five minutes.

You'll need

  • The Golf Companion phone app. Ask Kino for the invite link.
  • Garmin Connect on the same phone, with your watch paired to it.
  • A computer and your watch's charging cable.

1Download the file for your watch

Your model is on the back of the watch, or on the watch under Settings → System → About. Each watch needs its own file, so pick the exact model.

Loading the list of watches…

2Copy it onto the watch

Plug the watch into the computer with its charging cable. The file you downloaded is called golf_app.prg.

Windows
  1. Open File Explorer and click This PC. Your watch shows up either as a drive called GARMIN or under its own name.
  2. Open it, then Internal Storage if you see it, then the GARMIN folder, then Apps.
  3. Drag golf_app.prg into Apps. If it asks, choose to replace the file that's there.
Mac
  1. Older watches show up in Finder as a drive called GARMIN. Most newer ones don't. For those, install the free app OpenMTP and open it with the watch plugged in.
  2. Go into GARMIN, then Apps.
  3. Drag golf_app.prg into Apps, replacing the old one if it asks.

3Unplug and play

  1. Eject the watch (Windows: the eject icon in File Explorer; Mac: eject in Finder, or just close OpenMTP), then unplug it. The watch takes a moment to install the app.
  2. On the watch, open the list of activities and apps (on most watches, press START or the top-right button) and choose Golf Companion.
  3. Have your phone nearby with Garmin Connect running: your courses come from the Golf Companion phone app, nearest first.

Updating

When there's a new version, come back here and do steps 1 and 2 again. It replaces the old app, and your settings stay. Make sure any finished rounds have reached your phone first.

If something's not right

  • The app isn't on the watch: check the file went straight into the Apps folder (not a folder inside it), and that you picked your exact model.
  • The watch shows an error when you open it: it's almost always the file for a different model. Download yours again and replace it.
  • No courses on the watch: open the Golf Companion phone app and add your course there, keep Garmin Connect running, then open the watch app again.
