Kino Meintjes ("we") makes Golf Companion, an Android app and a
Garmin Connect IQ watch app for recording golf rounds. This policy explains
what the apps collect, why, who else sees it, and how to have it deleted.

## What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address and password | Only if you create an account | To sign you in. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase Auth) and are never visible to us. |
| Display name and friend code | When you have an account | So friends can find you. Your name is only searchable by others if you switch that on. |
| Golf rounds (scores, putts, fairways, penalties, green fees, the date, tees, and shot positions from the watch) | When you save a round | To show your scorecards, statistics and handicap estimate, and — if you're signed in — to keep them in your account across phones. |
| Courses you add (name, hole positions, scorecard, ratings) | When you save a course | Courses join a shared library other golfers can use. |
| Your location | While the app is open, and while the watch app is recording a round | To give distances to the green and hazards, to sort courses nearest first, and for "find courses near me". Location is used on your phone and watch; it is not stored in your account, except as the positions of shots the watch detected, which are stored with the round. |
| Photos of scorecards | Only when you choose to read a scorecard from a photo | The photo is sent to an AI service to read the card, then discarded; it is not kept. |
| A record of each scorecard lookup | When you use the scorecard lookup | To limit lookups per person per day. |

We do not collect contacts, browsing history, advertising identifiers or
payment details, and the app shows no advertising.

## Who else sees your data

- **Friends you accept** see your display name, handicap estimate,
  leaderboard figures and your three most recent rounds. Players in a game
  you join see that game's scorecards.
- **Service providers** who run parts of the app for us:
  - Supabase (database and sign-in), where account data is stored.
    West Europe (London)
  - OpenStreetMap services (map tiles, Nominatim and Overpass) receive the
    area being searched or shown — which can reveal your approximate
    location — when you search for courses, view maps or load hazards.
  - Open-Meteo receives a course's location to return its weather.
  - Google (Gemini) receive a club's web pages or a photo you took, to 
    read a scorecard.
  - Garmin carries messages between your phone and watch through Garmin
    Connect, under Garmin's own privacy policy.
- We never sell personal data, and share it with nobody else unless the law
  requires it.

## How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Rounds you save without
an account stay only on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the
app. Lookup records are kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically.

## Your choices and rights

- **Export:** Account tab → Your data → Export rounds (a spreadsheet file).
- **Delete:** Account tab → Delete my account removes your account and
  everything in it (rounds, handicap history, friends, games) permanently.
  Courses you added to the shared library stay, without your name. You can
  also ask us to delete your account at kinomeintjes@gmail.com.
- **Correct:** edit any round or course in the app.
- Under POPIA and GDPR you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct
  or delete it, or object to how we use it — contact kinomeintjes@gmail.com. You may
  also complain to South Africa's Information Regulator.

## Children

Golf Companion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect their data.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Account data is protected by
per-user access rules in the database, so one account can't read another's
rounds.

## Changes

We'll update this page when the apps change what they collect, and change the
date at the top.
